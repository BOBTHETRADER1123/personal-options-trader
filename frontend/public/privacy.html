Personal Options Trader

Privacy information

Updated 25 October 2026. Personal Options Trader is a private, owner-operated paper-trading workspace. It simulates trades and does not place real broker orders.

Information used

The workspace stores its owner's account settings, simulated trades, market-price timestamps, profit and loss records, reports and backup status. Broker credentials and Google authorization credentials are handled on the backend, not in public frontend code.

Google Drive and Sheets

Google authorization is optional. The app requests access only to Google Drive files it creates or that the owner selects for this app. It uses that access to create trading-report folders, synchronize daily Google Sheets, upload Excel and compressed CSV exports, verify copies and perform owner-authorized cleanup. It does not request access to Gmail, Photos or unrelated Drive files.

Offline authorization lets the cloud backend continue these tasks when the owner's devices are off. Google refresh tokens are stored encrypted on the backend. Credentials and tokens are excluded from trading exports.

Storage and service providers

The frontend is hosted on Vercel. The backend and primary SQLite records are hosted on RamNaym Cloud. Authorized Google reports are stored in the Google account selected by the owner. These providers process information as needed to operate those services. Google user data is not sold or used for advertising or AI model training.

Retention and control

The owner controls report downloads and cloud-copy cleanup. Automatic local archival removes eligible historical snapshot detail only after required exports and Google copies pass verification; essential trading state and continuity records are retained. Changing the connected Google account leaves existing files in the previous account.

The owner can revoke Google access at Google Account connections. Revoking access stops future authorized synchronization; existing reports remain until separately removed. Downloaded copies are under the owner's control.
